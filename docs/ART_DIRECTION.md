# Art direction

Toile uses warm paper, cobalt structural ink, faded distance ink, and one earthen accent. Strong contours describe structure; secondary lines describe form; fine marks supply texture; distant planes lose contrast and density. Typography pairs a literary display serif with compact sans-serif labels and monospaced code.

Illustration is never a wallpaper behind an unrelated card. Interactive artwork is decomposed by meaning—facade, shutter, window, vine, foreground—so state can travel in both directions between the drawing and semantic controls. Responsive editions are recomposed and cropped, not uniformly scaled.

Avoid rounded SaaS cards, gradients, glass, generic icon sets, ornamental blobs, synchronized motion, and faux-vintage clutter.
